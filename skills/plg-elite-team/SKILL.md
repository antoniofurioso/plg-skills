# Elite Team Flywheel (Design → Audit → Incentivize → Develop)

In a product-led company the product is the best employee (sales, support, onboarding), so hire as a last resort and track revenue per employee. Small teams can reach millions in ARR.

## 1. Design — accountability chart
Like an org chart, but one person can own several **jobs**; listing jobs separately stops you hunting for unicorns.
- Top: **Visionary** (ideas, culture, R&D) and **Integrator** (P&L, turning vision into plans). One person can hold both early on.
- Then: **Head of Go-to-Market** and **Head of Product**.
- Then every supporting job with its responsibilities (titles don't matter).
Check that the core capabilities from the One-Page Endgame (`plg-bullseye-strategy`) are covered; drop ones no longer needed.
Triage every job: **Trash** (eliminate) · **Transfer** (someone/something else does it better — includes automation/AI) · **Treasure** (critical; protect time for it). Usually 10–20% of jobs can go. Review each quarter.

## 2. Audit
**Quick audit (fit):** rate each person on each core value: always (+) · sometimes (+/–) · never (–). Repeated never/sometimes → part ways. Use also in interviews ("give an example of how you live X"), in candidate comparisons, in week 1–2 and before probation ends.
**Performance audit (per job, not per person):** for each job someone holds, rate Motivation · Results · Skills · Capacity. Then: keep, train (motivated but lacks skill), reassign (results but no motivation), or remove the job from them (no capacity). People wearing >3 hats need this most. Audit yourself first.

## 3. Incentivize
- **Individual:** ask each direct report for their 5-year vision (earnings, work, lifestyle, success). Connect daily work and feedback to it. If it doesn't fit the company, plan a respectful transition.
- **Team:** make winning shared — e.g. a profit pool share on hitting targets, rewards at progress markers toward the endgame. Something real at stake; the test is whether the team would feel crushed to miss it.

## 4. Develop
Monthly 30-min 1:1 with each direct report: pick ONE skill to build this month (ask: how did last month go? what do your metrics say? what skill would help most? how can I help?). Choose one high-impact resource (book, course, certification — ideally their pick). Check in weekly, coach with examples, treat it as a personal project reviewed in the monthly/weekly meetings.

## Output
Accountability chart (text or table) · Trash/Transfer/Treasure list · quick-audit and per-job audit tables · incentive plan · monthly development plan per person · recommendation on whether to hire, automate or reassign.
Solo founder version: list all jobs you hold, triage them, and decide what to automate, outsource or drop.

Source: *The Product-Led Playbook* (Wes Bush), ch. 9 (accountability chart concept from EOS; 3T from *Clockwork*). Unofficial summary.
