# Understand Desired Outcomes (JTBD)

Signing up is an act of hope: users believe life can be better. Onboarding is the job interview for the job they're hiring the product to do. Upgrade the user, not the product.

## What you need to learn
- The circumstance that triggered the search (the "job opening")
- The desired outcome and what success looks like
- What holds them back
- What else they tried or considered, and why it failed
- The #1 reason they chose this product

## The job has three parts
- **Functional** — the concrete outcome (not the drill, the hole — and what the hole is for). Varies by segment: one product can do different jobs for different users → segment onboarding ("What will you use this for?").
- **Emotional** — how they want to feel or avoid feeling.
- **Social** — how others will see them (look professional, be the hero at work).
Jobs are solution-agnostic: describe the need, not the product.

## The four forces of switching
- **Push** — pain of the current situation
- **Pull** — attraction of the new way
- **Anxiety** — fear about the new solution (won't work, migration, cost)
- **Habit/Inertia** — comfort of the old way
Onboarding must amplify push, strengthen pull, calm anxiety and break habit.

## Interview plan
Talk to five groups: new signups · evaluators/shoppers · active customers · paying-but-inactive · churned.
1. Recruit (in-app ask, email to segments; small thank-you).
2. Script — walk the timeline, ask for specifics, not opinions:
   - When did you first realise you needed something like this? What was happening?
   - What were you using before? What was frustrating about it?
   - What else did you look at? Why not those?
   - What almost stopped you from signing up/buying?
   - What did you expect to happen after signing up? What actually happened?
   - What does success look like a few months from now? How would others notice?
   - If this disappeared tomorrow, what would you do instead?
3. Conduct — 30 min, record, let silence work, follow the story.
4. Transcribe and tag quotes: trigger · push · pull · anxiety · habit · functional/emotional/social job · alternatives · success.
5. Synthesise patterns across interviews, focusing on core users who love the product.

If transcripts are provided, skip to steps 4–5 and quote evidence for every claim.

## Output: User Success Canvas
Per segment: what they see / feel / hear · functional, emotional, social job · push · pull · anxieties · habits · alternatives · #1 reason they chose you · implications for messaging (`plg-irresistible-offer`), First Strike (`plg-adoption-indicator`) and onboarding segments (`plg-bowling-alley-onboarding`).
