# Product-Led Sales (sales-assisted onboarding)

Being product-led isn't anti-sales. Sales-assisted conversion can be several times higher than pure self-serve across deal sizes, and most product-led companies add sales as they move upmarket.

## When sales belongs
| | Low buying complexity | High buying complexity |
|---|---|---|
| High contract value (≳$100k) | hybrid | sales-led |
| Mid ($2k–$100k) | hybrid | hybrid |
| Low | product-led | **avoid** — business won't survive |

Three jobs for sales in a product-led company:
1. Help users reach value (remove hidden blockers — more coach than closer)
2. Expand inside accounts (land and expand: one team → whole company; work at account level)
3. Guide complex buying (security reviews, procurement, custom terms)

How the role changes: from chasing leads to **coaching users** · framing value differently for end-users (time, ease) vs executives (ROI) · using **product engagement data** in every conversation.

## Define PQLs
1. Instrument the key milestones of the straight-line onboarding.
2. Criteria: activation (how far through onboarding) + engagement (frequency of core-feature use, features tried, speed to try them). Rule of thumb: completed onboarding + First Strike. Can sit anywhere between signup and the Product Adoption Indicator — more sales capacity → lower bar.
3. Rank users/accounts by how close they are (engagement score). In B2B, score **accounts**, not individuals.

## Customer fit (ICP)
List attributes that predict success: company size, industry, revenue, role, team/department size, budget, geography, tech stack, customer-base size, maturity. Derive them from your best customers: why they bought, what nearly stopped them, the goal, how they solved it before.

## Product-Led Qualification Matrix
- High engagement + good fit → **SQL**: sales engages.
- High engagement + poor fit → **PQL**: support, no sales push unless they ask.
- Low engagement + good fit → **"heartbreakers"**: nurture, offer help.
- Low engagement + poor fit → ignore / automate.

## When to reach out
- **Hand-raisers** (form, chat, feature request, email): respond like customer success — expert, helpful, no pressure.
- **Proactive**, for good-fit accounts only: right after signup (help set up properly / proof of concept) · after a win (congratulate, show the next step) · top-down once several people from one company are active (pitch to the executive with usage insights).
Never: three calls in the first 15 minutes, or a demo gate that blocks trying.

## Output
Model choice on the matrix · PQL definition and scoring table · ICP attributes · qualification matrix rules · outreach triggers with message drafts · what to track in the CRM.
