# PLG Preflight Checklist

Most PLG failures are readiness failures, not strategy failures. Five levels, built bottom-up. Score each 1–10. Below 7 = work on that level before the ones above it. The lowest score is the bottleneck.

## 1. Conviction — do the founders/CEO truly believe?
- Is PLG the only way to reach the vision, or one option alongside sales?
- Can you explain why PLG fits *without* mentioning competitors? (Peer pressure fades.)
- If results took 18 months, would you keep going?
Scale: 1 = curious · 5 = open but hedging · 10 = would bet the company.

## 2. Commitment — is the whole leadership behind it?
- Does every leader understand it, want it, and have capacity for it?
- Is it a top-3 company priority (not #7, which gets bumped every quarter)?
- Do incentives reward it? (Sales paid only on outbound closes and product measured on shipping velocity instead of activation = silent killer.)
- Willing to change culture from "celebrate closed deals" to "obsess over making the product easy and valuable"?
Scale: 1 = no alignment · 5 = approved but not top priority · 10 = full buy-in, top 3.

## 3. Capabilities — can the team actually do it?
- Could the team debate free trial vs freemium with reasons, not gut feel?
- Skills: UX designs for time-to-value (not just looks); data person tracks activation milestones, not only upgrades; marketing drives self-serve signups.
- Knowledge: can they state the activation metric right now, as a specific behaviour?
- Coverage: product marketing, product analytics, UX/onboarding, pricing, customer research, conversion optimization — where are the gaps?
Most fixable level: train, create a shared vocabulary, fill gaps with specialists.

## 4. Construction — does the product overdeliver?
- **Value gap** = promised (perceived) value minus experienced value. Big gap → leaky funnel and lost trust.
- Overdeliver on three axes: outcome quality ("wow" not "fine"), speed (time your own signup; the bar is now ~60 seconds to meaningful value), price (could they get the same outcome cheaper/easier elsewhere?).
Most teams score low here — that's expected; it's where the Playbook skills apply.

## 5. Cash — is PLG becoming the business?
- Does PLG drive or shape >50% of revenue? (Sales-closed deals that started in the product count.)
- Is PLG revenue growing month over month, or flat for 3+ months?
Scale: <4 normal early if rising · 4–7 danger zone (alive but not permanent — don't coast) · >7 it's the engine, protect it. Install a weekly rhythm: biggest bottleneck → experiments → measure.

## Remedy by lowest level
- Conviction → decide whether the vision truly needs PLG; research, talk to companies that switched.
- Commitment → stop building; hard conversation with leadership on priority and incentives.
- Capabilities → invest in team before product; shared language.
- Construction → run the Playbook in order: `plg-bullseye-strategy` → `plg-user-endgame` → `plg-free-model` → `plg-irresistible-offer` → `plg-bowling-alley-onboarding` → `plg-value-ladder-pricing`.
- Cash → `plg-true-north-scorecard` + `plg-growth-process`.

## Output
Table of five scores with evidence → bottleneck → 3 concrete actions for that level → what not to work on yet.

Source: *Product-Led Growth* (Wes Bush, 2026 ed.), ch. 6. Unofficial summary.
