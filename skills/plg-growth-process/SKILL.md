# Predictable Growth Process

Execution beats being first. Three recurring meetings turn strategy (`plg-bullseye-strategy`) and the scorecard (`plg-true-north-scorecard`) into focused work. Speed up the rhythm in tough times; slow it when calm.
Recommendation: have someone else facilitate quarterly and monthly sessions so leaders can think.

Scorecard statuses used everywhere: on track · trending on track · off track, have plan · off track, no plan · off track, no plan, unreachable. Off track without a plan → goes on the issues list.

## Quarterly — Strategic Alignment Meeting (full day, leadership ≤10; replaces that month's monthly meeting)
Prep: One-Page Endgame, scorecard with quarter results, list of priorities/issues.
1. Check-in: expectations for today (15 min)
2. Retro: what went well / didn't / do differently (30)
3. Strategy review: go through the One-Page Endgame section by section — still the best path? (30)
4. Accountability chart review (30; from the second cycle — see `plg-elite-team`)
5. Scorecard review (15)
6. Targets: top outcomes (revenue, revenue per employee, profit) and scorecard targets that make the yearly/quarterly picture inevitable — agreed cross-functionally (10)
7. 3–5 SMART quarterly goals (annual goals once a year) (20)
8. Issues: discuss and solve (90)
9. Deep dive on ONE system component that needs a refresh (strategy, user, offer…) (30)
10. ONE top focus for next month = highest-leverage opportunity; the scorecard bottleneck is a clue, not the answer — also look at what's *not* being done (30)
11. Top projects for the focus: owner, who executes, ≤5 per person, into the project tool (30)
12. Cascade: all-hands/recap so everyone knows the focus (5)
13. Close: expectations met? rate 1–10, feedback (5)

## Monthly — Focus Meeting (half day, leadership)
Prep: updated scorecard, project status, issues.
Retro, answers prepared beforehand (15) · review last month's focus and projects — not done by month end = off track; did we dent the focus? wrong bet or weak execution? (30) · scorecard (15) · update Monthly Picture + targets (10) · issues (45) · ONE top focus (10) · top projects ranked by impact on focus/targets and cost; owner, executors, scope; ≤5 per person; killing projects is fine (20) · cascade (5) · rate (5).

## Weekly — Accelerator Meeting (90 min, leadership)
Wins, one personal + one professional (5) · monthly picture on/off (3) · scorecard on/off (3) · projects on/off (3) · last week's to-dos done? (3) · issues: triage, find root causes, solve (60) · each person's top 3 priorities + to-dos (10) · rate (3).

## Experiments inside the rhythm
For each project: hypothesis → input metric it should move → smallest shippable version → measure → share learnings company-wide (wins channel, recap email, monthly demo).

## Output
Calendar of recurring meetings · filled agendas/templates for the next quarterly, monthly and weekly sessions · this month's single focus + project list with owners.
For a solo founder: compress to a monthly 2-hour self-review and a 30-minute weekly check using the same agenda.

Source: *The Product-Led Playbook* (Wes Bush), ch. 8 (+ Triple-A loop from *Product-Led Onboarding* ch. 9). Unofficial summary.
